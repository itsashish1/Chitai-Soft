WHAT WE DO

Our Services

End-to-end automation engineering — from control architecture design through commissioning, integration, and ongoing support.

⋄ CONTROL ARCHITECTURE

PLC & SCADA Systems

We design, programme, and commission PLC-based control systems and SCADA platforms for industrial plants. Our engineers work across all major automation vendors — delivering systems that are reliable, maintainable, and built to your process requirements.

PLC Programming & Panel Design

Full-cycle PLC development across multiple platforms and vendors. We handle I/O mapping, ladder logic, function block diagrams, and structured text — alongside control panel design, wiring, and factory acceptance testing.

SCADA Development & HMI

Custom SCADA applications and operator interfaces that give your team clear, actionable visibility — with real-time process graphics, alarm management, trend displays, and role-based access control.

Historian & Data Integration

Process data historian setup and integration with plant-level databases. We configure OPC-UA and Modbus communication layers so your operational data flows reliably to where it is needed.

Remote Access & Diagnostics

Secure remote access infrastructure for off-site monitoring and fault diagnosis. Reduce call-out costs and mean-time-to-repair with always-on visibility into your control systems.

Our Delivery Process

01 Requirements & Architecture

We review your process, define control philosophy, and architect the optimal hardware/software configuration.

02 Development & Testing

PLC programmes and SCADA screens are built, configured, and simulated thoroughly in our engineering lab (FAT).

03 Installation & Commissioning

Our engineers install, wire, and commission on-site to ensure seamless integration and validation (SAT).

04 Handover & Support

Full documentation, operator training, and post-commissioning support to maintain peak operational uptime.

⋄ INDUSTRY 4.0

Industrial IoT & Smart Manufacturing

Connect your plant floor to the cloud without replacing existing equipment. We deploy edge computing infrastructure, sensor networks, and analytics platforms that give plant managers live OEE data, predictive maintenance alerts, and production KPIs — turning raw machine data into decisions.

Edge Computing & Gateway Deployment

Industrial edge gateways installed at machine level to collect, pre-process, and securely transmit data from PLCs, sensors, and legacy equipment. We handle protocol translation (OPC-UA, MQTT, Modbus) and ensure data integrity from source to cloud.

OEE & Production Analytics

Real-time Overall Equipment Effectiveness dashboards that track availability, performance, and quality across your production lines. Identify your biggest losses, benchmark shifts, and drive continuous improvement with data your team can trust.

Predictive Maintenance

Vibration, temperature, and current-signature monitoring on critical rotating assets — motors, pumps, compressors. Machine-learning models flag anomalies before they become failures, reducing unplanned downtime and extending asset life.
