OUR STORY

Built on Engineering Expertise

Chitai Soft Automation was founded with a single purpose — to bring dependable, well-engineered automation to Indian industry. We work with plant operators, project managers, and engineering teams across the country to design, commission, and support industrial control systems that perform.

From our early projects in power generation to our current work across oil & gas, water treatment, and process industries, we have built a reputation for technical rigour and straightforward delivery. We do not over-promise. We scope carefully, engineer thoroughly, and stand behind our work.

Our team combines hands-on site experience with deep knowledge of PLC programming, SCADA development, and Industrial IoT — giving clients a single point of accountability from design through commissioning and beyond.

Technical Rigour

Every system we deliver is designed to specification, tested thoroughly, and documented completely. No shortcuts.

Site-First Thinking

Our engineers have spent time on plant floors. We design for the realities of industrial environments, not just the drawing board.

Vendor Independence

We work across all major automation platforms and recommend what is right for your process — not what is convenient for us.

Long-Term Support

We build relationships, not just systems. Our clients know they can call us after commissioning and we will be there.
